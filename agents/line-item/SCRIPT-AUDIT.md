# Script audit: low-poly stories (2026-10-28)

Benchmark: **Penny got 1,685 IG views in ~14 h** (the old format: 109–161). What it did right is the template.

## What makes a script work (the checklist every script must pass)
1. **Line 1 = the whole hook**: a surprising, true, concrete claim with a number, under 10 words, said in the first 2 s. ("Every penny America made cost almost four cents.")
2. **No soft line in the first 5 s**: no scene-setting before the claim.
3. **Escalate**: every beat raises the stakes or adds a new fact. A list of features ("Zinc inside. A thin skin of copper...") is a dip; keep it to one line.
4. **Re-hook at ~40%**: a question the viewer now needs answered ("So what happened?").
5. **Twist after the re-hook**: something they didn't see coming (Treasury said no → bottles came out empty).
6. **Payoff + loop**: the last line must run grammatically into line 1 ("...which is why" → "for 73 years, a Coke cost exactly one nickel"), and the last frame matches the first. A replay then feels seamless and gets counted as a rewatch.
7. Max 4 figures, every claim sourced, 30–40 s, calm read.
