# Line Item: new-angle research (YouTube Shorts data, pulled 2026-10-26)

Method: yt-dlp channel Shorts tabs (view counts from flat listing, upload dates from per-video pulls), YouTube search / channel search. ~60 calls. TikTok/Reels not measurable with yt-dlp; web write-ups found were vendor blogs (weak evidence, only used to find leads: Autonolab posts named Big Data Factor / InfoGeek). All numbers are snapshots; subs are YouTube's rounded figures.

## Headline findings
1. Our current angle (price/"hidden cost" breakdown explainer, text+VO) is the saturated, low-reach zone. Channel search for "price breakdown / cost of everything / hidden costs / how much does it cost" returns dozens of micro-channels. Ones I pulled: The Cost of Everything Shorts (32 shorts, median 0, max 1,400), Price X-Ray (140), Silent Markup HQ (max 6), AssetsXposed (13 shorts, max 6), Price Autopsy (4). Same ~100-views result as ours. It is the topic framing, not our execution.
2. What breaks out right now is a VISUAL object doing the explaining (3D map, 3D mechanism, low-poly character story), one idea per video, 20-45 s, with a curiosity or comparison title. Topic matters less than the visual device.
3. Three distinct breakouts in the last 90 days, all verified with dates (below).

## Candidate angles

### A. 3D map country-vs-country comparison (money/prices/rules)  <- recommended #1
Channel: Shivang Verse (youtube.com/@ShivangVerse area, UCsPlXI1uMPyj4fK0JY5v6OA). ~101k subs, 791 Shorts, joined 2023 but old content was space/facts at median ~12-15k views. Pivoted to "3D Map Animation" Aug/Sep 2026.
Last 40 Shorts (13 Sep-26 Oct 2026, ~1/day): median 143k, max 2.0M. Pre-pivot median 12-15k (items 40-200). Same channel, same audience, ~10x lift from the format alone.
Examples (all Sep/Oct 2026, 21-34 s):
- India vs Pakistan Explained | 3D Map Animation: 2.0M (vIIlkRbDzv4, 2 Oct)
- Indian Rs500 vs Pakistan vs USA vs Japan vs Nepal (currency notes): 1.83M (VB9RJADsUg4, 27 Sep)
- India vs USA vs Japan: Government Schools: 1.4M (pf9pQxjWzfc)
- Rs100 in India vs Pakistan vs USA vs Nepal vs China: 169k (uUzIAEE5p3I, 7 Oct) ; PM & President Salary Comparison 161k (JN0jSu9QbQM, 10 Oct)
Format: ~21-26 s, 9:16, a 3D globe/map that hops country to country, one flag/object per country, big title "X vs Y vs Z | 3D Map Animation", very fast beat (1 country per 3-4 s), VO or short text per stop. Hook = the comparison in the title/thumbnail; payoff is "where does my country land".
Why it travels: national pride/outrage, comment wars ("where is X?"), shares to friends from that country, rewatch because it is 22 s.
Fit: 5/5 (Three.js globe/map, flags as textures, kinetic type; all code). Money cuts: salaries, price of same item, currency notes (banknote images = careful, use only own-drawn/PD), minimum wage, rent, tax.
Saturation: in English, search for "3D map animation comparison countries" returns only micro channels (<100 subs). The big cluster is Hindi/India-focused (also WorldTV Studio, 707k subs, "Aircraft/Tanks Fleet Strength by Country", median 30k, max 317k, and Global Data 797k subs). So audience skew is India/Pakistan nationalism, which is partly what drives the numbers. Risk: the top hits depend on India-Pakistan rivalry; the neutral topics (Breakfast 26k, Famous Foods 29k, Online payment apps 12k) are 10x weaker. Pick topics with a rivalry/outrage/"I did not know" edge.

### B. Low-poly 3D one-story Shorts (weird true stories, with a mechanism)  <- recommended #2 (strongest raw numbers, weaker brand fit)
Channels (all started in 2026, i.e. true cold starts):
- Low Poly Shorts: 1.24M subs, only 91 Shorts, median ~6.9M on last 40, max 53.2M ("You've Never Touched Anything", 14 Feb 2026), 24.8M ("Why You Shouldn't Point Lasers At Planes", 1 Mar 2026), 12.9M (13 Jun 2026); latest (23 Oct 2026) "You May Have An Evil Twin" 1.16M within 3 days.
- LowPolyPaws: 167k subs, 97 Shorts, first upload ~Jul 2026 (510k), peak 13.9M "How Chubby Cats Lose Weight" (3 Oct 2026), median ~2.0M.
- 3D Poly Films: 147k subs, 155 Shorts, first ~Feb 2026 (4.0M), "If You Delete This Gene, You Become A Monster" 2.7M (4 Sep 2026), median 278k.
Format: 30-57 s, low-poly characters/objects, simple lighting, a calm explanatory VO (auto-captions show natural speech ~2.5 words/s), title = a "Why you shouldn't..." / "He ...for 124 days" curiosity gap; a human/animal/object in a real incident, then the mechanism ("atoms repel via electromagnetic force"). Hook line is the first sentence, spoken at 0.2 s. No music observed in metadata (cannot confirm by ear).
Why it travels: self-contained mini-story with a surprising true fact, lots of "wait what" comments, high completion because it resolves in 40 s.
Fit: 3/5 (Three.js can do low-poly primitives and simple rigs; characters/animation are the real cost; Fish TTS fine). Brand fit only if stories are money-themed ("Low IQ Bank Robbers" 2.5M, "Dumbest Burglars" 10M show crime/money works).
Saturation: clone search "low poly 3d animation facts shorts" gives ~15 micro channels (<1k subs) and only 3 above 100k. High reach ceiling, hard to execute at quality; most clones fail.

A1. "Minimum Wage Around the World | 3D Map". Hook (0-2 s): "One hour of work pays this much in each country." B1. "Why The Penny Cost 3.7 Cents To Make". Hook: "The U.S. lost money on every single penny." Low-poly coin and Mint machine; US Mint FY2024 cost to make a penny 3.69 cents, production ended in 2025 per the Treasury announcement. Travel: counterintuitive, shareable, resolves in 35 s.
B2. "The Man Who Sold The Eiffel Tower. Twice." Hook: "He sold the Eiffel Tower and then did it again." Low-poly Paris and a scrap-dealer figure; Victor Lustig, 1925 scam, documented, he sold it to a scrap dealer (Andre Poisson) and returned to Paris to repeat it before fleeing. Travel: crime + money story, rewatch for the twist, consistent with Line Item's money brand.

## Production notes tied to evidence
- Hook text/voice in the first 0.2 s with a concrete claim; 21-26 s for the map format, 35-57 s for story format.
- One idea per video; title carries the comparison or the curiosity gap.
- Do not copy named IP (Attack on Titan etc.). Use your own flag/currency graphics; avoid photographed banknotes.
- Disclose synthetic voice if the platform requires it.
- Caveat: Shivang Verse's top hits are India/Pakistan rivalry; neutral topics did 10x worse. Test with rivalry/"outrage-adjacent" money pairs (e.g., your country vs neighbour) not generic ones.
