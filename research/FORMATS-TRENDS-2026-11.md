## 3. Getting the right eyes
Evidence is thin on UAE specifics. Almost nothing measures Instagram for UAE small-business owners; treat that part as hypotheses to test in the first 4–6 weeks.

- **Hashtags:** Instagram is capping at 5 (Social Media Today 2025-12-18, B). Metricool (24.4M posts, 2026-06-16, B): posts with a hashtag got fewer views, but this is confounded. Use 3–5 as labels; keywords matter more.
- **IG SEO:** public professional-account posts can appear in Google since 2025-07-10 (via Metricool, B). **No official Instagram text found** on caption, on-screen text or alt text as ranking inputs; vendors claim it (C). Cheap and harmless: keywords in Name field, bio, first caption line, on-screen text and speech. Skip watermarked exports (Instagram's recommendation guidance, A, but 2022).
- **UAE geo:** no source measures Arabic vs English reach. Strongest available signals: AED figures, WhatsApp, local terms (RERA/DLD, DHA/DOH, trade licence, free zone), "Dubai, United Arab Emirates" location tag. NapoleonCat: ~9.8M UAE Instagram users, ~67% men (2026-04, B-/C).
- **Collabs:** one 100-post academic study (B-): reach tracks a weighted average of both accounts, so a zero-follower account gains little from an irrelevant one. Best partners would be UAE business consultants, PRO/licensing firms, accountants, CRM/WhatsApp agencies, coworking spaces. Outreach is Nathan's.
- **"Comment LEAK" CTAs:** Instagram said in June 2024 that explicit "comment X" asks won't be recommended, then clarified that lead-gen via comments/DMs with tools like ManyChat is not its target (SMT 2024-06-05, B). Real risk of reduced non-follower reach on bait-looking posts. Make the CTA a concrete offer and mix in posts without it.
- **DM rules (A):** private reply within 7 days, one message per commenter, follow-up only after they reply and within 24 h. Non-followers land in Message Requests. Benchmarks are vendor-only (Inro: link in second message 42% vs 29%; first message <120 characters; one button), grade C; no independent comment-to-DM benchmark exists.
- **Reels vs carousel for small accounts:** Fanpage Karma (3M posts, 2026): under 10K followers, Reels reach ~3x as many non-followers as carousels. Socialinsider (67.5M posts): carousels get more views at 1–10K. Different metrics. For zero followers, Reels for discovery plus one weekly carousel for saves (Metricool: carousels 9x saves) is a sensible test. Shorts not covered.
- **Posting times:** UAE federal week is Mon–Thu plus half-day Fri since 2022 (WAM, A), but the private sector isn't mandated. All posting-time sources are modelled or agency opinion (C/D). Test Tue–Thu 7:30–9am, 12:30–2pm, 8–10pm Dubai time; Fridays before ~1pm or after ~7pm. Most views come in the first 3 days (Metricool), so the hook matters more than the slot.

## 4. Script style
**No tone wins outright, and the evidence is thin.**
- **Funny/skit:** biggest reach (100K–2.1M from small accounts: bigwaveclean 291K from 746 followers, mianoorrealestatellc 2.1M with 73 comments) but low comment rates (mostly 0.003–0.09%), and audiences are often peers. Closest match to us: bijayini_rathh agency-owner skit, 81K, 41 comments.
- **Witty/dry:** skit-level reach; anastasiia_gurskaya (Dubai) had the best skit comment rate at 0.19%. The only conversion figures are vendor-claimed (Vector: +22% demo requests, +44% pipeline, grade C).
- **Plain professional:** less reach, but with an explicit "comment X" CTA it showed the highest comment rates in the set (0.36–0.40%).
- **Studies:** Swani et al. 2025 (J. Business Research, 4 experiments, n=305, A, small): humor helps B2B ads when it relates to the product and is weaker when unrelated or the buyer already has a relationship. No independent study covers tone for short-form aimed at small-business owners. Practitioners agree skits drive reach and trust drives conversion (Sweetfish 2026-05, C).
- **UAE:** formality is common but varies by emirate and sector; English carries business, with Arabic touches (trade.gov, B; HiDubai, C). Expats are fragmented, so a joke can miss. My inference: joke about the process ("nobody answers the phone"), never about the owner.
- **Test first:** plain professional diagnosis plus one deadpan line tied to the leak, against a plain control and one skit arm, same leaks across all three. Measure per 1,000 views: LEAK comments, DMs, free checks completed, purchases. Count qualified UAE owners, not likes. Skits usually need faces, which favours voiceover and numbers for us.
