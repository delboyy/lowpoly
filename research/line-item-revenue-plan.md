## 2. Projection (steady-state month, 1 post/day, USD)
**Assumptions:** median views per Short is the YouTube figure; mean views = 2× median (Shorts are heavy-tailed; revenue and YPP thresholds follow the mean); Instagram and TikTok each get 50% of YouTube's views; 30 posts a month per platform; Shorts RPM mid $0.05 (range $0.03–0.10); sponsor CPM mid $17.5 on median views, 2 sponsored Shorts/month at 100k and 1M, 1 at 10k, with a 50% chance each deal closes; products/affiliate at $0.10 per 1k views across all platforms.

|---|---|---|---|

| All-platform views/month | 600k + 2 × 300k = 1.2M | 12M | 120M |

| **Total / month** | **~$210** | **~$3,250** | **~$32,500** |

Notes on the arithmetic: Whop net = $1.50 headline × 0.75 (possible 25% early fee, unverified, see `revenue-analyst.md`) × 60% payable share ≈ $0.68 per 1k. Sponsor and product lines depend on closing deals and building a product, so the 1M column is an upper bound, not a forecast. UAE audiences are valuable for ads: a tracker puts UAE YouTube RPM near $3.2 per 1k (all formats), above the median market (Dynamoi, 2026, aggregated), but no Shorts-specific figure exists. **Tax:** Google withholds US tax on US-viewer revenue at 30% when the creator's country has no treaty; the UAE has none with the US (my knowledge, not confirmed by YouTube's page) while the UK rate is reduced under the treaty. At ~40% US viewers that is roughly a 12% cut on ad income. Nathan should settle his tax residency before filing the AdSense tax form (pbl.legal and AIR, 2026).

## 3. Set up now so a breakout isn't wasted
**Anyone can do (agents): ** a "business inquiries" email in the YouTube/IG bio; a one-page link-in-bio with a free newsletter signup; the first spreadsheet product built from a story's numbers; a sponsor one-pager (views, geo, topic, rates); disclosure habit ("Ad" first for any sponsored or affiliate content).
**Only Nathan can do:**
1. **YouTube Studio → Earn → "Get notified"** and turn on 2-Step Verification; create the AdSense account (PIN may be posted to the address on file).
2. **Decide tax residency** (UAE vs the UK address used earlier) and fill the YouTube US tax form accordingly; ask an accountant. The wrong country costs up to 30% of US-viewer revenue.
3. **Apply to YPP before 23:59 UTC on 2027-01-31** the moment the channel has 1,000 subscribers and 10M Shorts views in 90 days (the AIR guide says the date that counts is when the application reaches YouTube). After that the bar is 20M.
4. Confirm a payout route to a UAE bank for the store (Gumroad/Payhip/Stripe) before launching a product.
5. Instagram: keep the account Professional, check the Gifts tile once (UAE unlisted, so expect none).

## 4. Length and format
- **Keep Shorts at 30–40s.** TikTok's 1-minute rule only matters for Creator Rewards, which UAE accounts can't join; Shorts ad share is the same per view at any length. Retention beats length here.
- **Do not make long compilations yet.** Long-form pays ~$2 per 1k views (AIR median, 300 channels) against ~$0.05 for Shorts, so one 100k-view compilation is ~$200 vs ~$5 for the same views as a Short, and its watch hours also count towards YPP (Shorts-feed hours do not). Start a weekly 8–10 minute compilation when **three Shorts have passed 500k views** (proves demand) or YPP is reached; use the best-performing stories with new connective narration so it is not a repeat of the Shorts (YouTube's reused-content and template rules).
- Vary the story structure so a recurring look does not trigger the "inauthentic/template" check (YouTube Help 1311392).

## 5. Risks
- **Regulated finance:** keep affiliates and sponsors off credit, investments and crypto (UK FCA s.21 FSMA/FG24/1 was my earlier finding; check UAE rules if promoting UAE audiences' financial products).
- **Views assumptions are untested:** the old format got ~140 views; every column above assumes the new format works. Re-baseline after 20 posts.
- **Instagram/TikTok give no direct pay to a UAE account;** their value is traffic to YouTube and the link in bio.
- **Sources:** YouTube Help and blog, Instagram Help/creators site (read 2026-10-27); vidIQ, AIR Media-Tech, Dynamoi, pbl.legal, sponsor rate cards (vendor, 2026). The Low Poly Shorts benchmarks (1.24M subs, 6.9M median) came from the Line Item chat and were not re-verified by me; I found no public source for that channel.
