## 5. Q5 · Honest numbers vs v4
`monthly $ = new setups + MRR clients × fee + builds × ~$1,000 + Cobundle` (gross; minutes cost ~$65–155 per receptionist a month)
| Case [A] | 90 days (Nov–Jan) | 12 months (to 2027-10-31) | $10k cumulative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pessimistic: 1 MRR client in Mar, 1 Shopify sprint | **~$0–300** | **~$3k** | not by Oct 2027 |
| Base: 1 sprint in Dec; MRR clients added Feb, May, Aug; 3 other builds in the year | **~$1,000** | **~$9k** ($4.5k MRR + $0.9k setups + $0.9k sprint + ~$3k builds) | ~Nov–Dec 2027 |
| Good: 2 receptionists by Jan, then 1 a month to ~8; 1 build a quarter | **~$3,000** | **~$20k** (~59 client-months) | ~Jun 2027 |
| **v4 Store Leaks (Red Team-adjusted)** | $300–1,300 | $2–5k | ~15–20% by Oct 2027 |
| **v5 Business Leaks** | **$0–3,000** | **$3–20k** | **~25–30% by Oct 2027** [M] |
**Verdict:**
- **Broadening barely changes the 90 days.** Clients 1–3 come from warm reach either way, and intro prices are low.
- **It raises the 12 months,** because the MRR offer compounds and the warm pool is no longer limited to Shopify owners.
- **Costs:**
  - The content is split across two series.
  - There's no non-Shopify case study yet.
  - Every receptionist client adds about 1 h/month of support and the liability for a dropped call.
  - **Capacity caps the good case at ~8 MRR clients plus 1 build a quarter** on 2–3 h/week. Beyond that, Nathan needs more hours or a raise to standard prices.
