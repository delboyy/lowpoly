## 0. The rule that picks the niche
With tiny views, the content won't sell a $49 product to strangers in volume (R2: ~$0.01–0.06 per 1k views for a low-ticket product). So the niche must have **(a) buyers we can reach without views** (Nathan's clients, Cobundle merchants, Shopify communities), **(b) a $150–1,000 offer Nathan can deliver mostly with agents inside 2–3 h/week**, and **(c) content that is better in low-poly than talking head**. The content's job is trust, samples and a slowly growing list. The first sales come from warm reach.

## 2. N1 "Store Leaks": the plan
**Angle:** every video is one real leak in a Shopify store, shown as a low-poly world (a cart with holes, a checkout corridor with doors closing), with a number hook, a twist and a loop. The fix ends the video, and the fix is something we sell.
**Brand:** rename @lineitemfyi → e.g. "Store Leaks" [V handle/trademark]. The low-poly money-story style stays; the audience is now store owners.

### 2.1 Five hooks (number in line 1, twist, loop ending)
1. **"About 7 in 10 Shopify carts never reach checkout."** (Baymard ~70% across 49 studies [V].) Twist: the #1 reason isn't price, it's a surprise cost at the last step. Loop: the cart rolls back to the shelf → line 1.
2. **"53% of mobile visitors leave if a page takes over 3 seconds."** (Google, 2016 [V; label the date].) Twist: they never see your product, so your ads paid for nothing. Loop: the loading ring closes back into frame 1.
3. **"Up to 35% of Amazon's sales come from 'you may also like'."** (McKinsey estimate [V].) Twist: a small store's version is a bundle, and most stores don't offer one. Loop: one box becomes two, then back to one.
4. **"Most stores convert about 2 in 100 visitors."** (Find a primary source, or drop [V].) Twist: you don't need double the traffic; one more buyer per 100 is +50%. Loop: visitor 100 walks back to the door.
5. **"1 free Shopify Flow rule can tag every repeat buyer for you."** (Check plan availability [V].) Twist: most stores never set it up, so their best customers get the same emails as everyone else. Loop: the tag drops on the next order. (This is the N3 tier.)
Cadence: 4 Shorts/week on IG, YT and TikTok, ~1 h each with a store-world scene pack built once (~1 day).

### 2.3 Funnel and first buyers
- **Route A, warm (no views needed). These are the first buyers:**
  1. **Nathan's 2 clients:** offer a free Leak Report to one as the case study, then the sprint.
  2. **Cobundle's existing merchants:** an in-app or email note, "free Leak Report for the first 10" (Nathan sends; install count pending).
  3. **Shopify community spaces** (Shopify Community forum, r/shopify, Shopify Partners): Nathan posts one case study with its low-poly video. No spam; his account, his call.
- **Route B, content:** Shorts → "comment LEAKS" or bio link → free checklist → email (5-email sequence) → $49 Playbook → Leak Report → sprint. At R2 rates this is slow: ~20k views/month × 0.0025–0.1% ≈ 0.5–20 sign-ups/month at first. **Treat it as compounding, not as the first sale.**
- **Tracking:** UTM link and code per video and per route, plus one weekly line in `plan/` (views, sign-ups, sales, reports, sprints).

### 2.4 Arithmetic (formulas, then ranges)
`monthly $ = Leak Reports × $295 + sprints × $900 + Playbooks × $45 + Cobundle MRR`
| | 90 days (Nov 2026–Jan 2027) | 12 months (to Oct 2027) |
|---|---|---|
| Pessimistic | 1 report at $195 + 3 Playbooks ≈ **$330** | ~$2k (reports stall; Route B never grows) |
| Base [A] | 4 reports ($195→295) + 1 sprint + 10 Playbooks ≈ **$2,300** | 2 reports/mo + 1 sprint every 2 months + 15 Playbooks/mo (as the list grows) ≈ $1,800/mo by month 6 → **~$10k cumulative around Aug–Sep 2027** |
| Good [A] | 8 reports + 2 sprints + 25 Playbooks ≈ **$5,000** | 4 reports/mo + 1 sprint/mo + 30 Playbooks/mo ≈ $3,500/mo → **$10k by ~Apr 2027** |
How the warm conversions are built: 10 free reports to Cobundle merchants + 2 clients → 2–3 buy a sprint or a paid report from someone they refer [A]. 30 posts plus community case studies → 50–150 list sign-ups by month 3 [A] → 2–5% buy the Playbook [R1: cold 0.5–1%, warm list higher] → 1–7 sales.
**Nathan's capacity limits it:** 2–3 h/week ≈ 10 h/month = ~6 reports reviewed + 0 sprints, or 2 reports + 1 sprint. If sprints sell, his hours (or an agent-heavy sprint) are the constraint. That's a good problem; raise the price.

### 2.5 Hours and costs
- **Nathan:** 2–3 h/week (report reviews, warm sends and one community post a week; sprints are extra and paid).
- **Us:** 4 Shorts/week, the checklist and Playbook (build ~2 days, once), the Leak Report pipeline (public-page checks + a templated low-poly leak video, ~2–3 h per report [A]; meter the first one).
- **Cost:** ~$0–30/month (email tier, store fees 5–10%).

### 2.6 Kill criteria (dated)
1. **2026-12-15:** if 10 free Leak Reports offered to Cobundle merchants and clients get **<3 takers**, the offer is wrong → change it (e.g. a speed-only report) before building more.
2. **2027-01-31:** if there are **0 paid reports or sprints**, stop the services rungs and keep only content + Playbook.
3. **2027-01-31:** if there are **<50 email sign-ups after ~50 Shorts**, the content isn't building a buying audience → keep Shorts only as report samples and stop optimising for reach.
4. **2027-03-31:** if total revenue is **<$1,000**, N1 won't reach $10k in 12 months; the fallback is Nathan's hourly store work plus Cobundle, with Lowpoly as their marketing.

## 3. Why N1 over N2/N3, and what's still unknown
- **N1 wins on all three rules in §0:**
  - Reachable buyers without views (Nathan's clients and Cobundle merchants).
  - An agent-made, priced-at-market offer that fits his hours (the Leak Report).
  - A visual edge in an empty lane (no 3D Shopify-growth creator found).
- It also has the only real product signal we found: a $49 checklist with 129 ratings.
- N2 fights saturated, free-template competitors and needs a community-led face. N3 is a service tier inside N1, not a separate brand.
- **Unknowns that change the ranges:**
  - Cobundle's installs and price (decides Route A's size).
  - Whether Nathan will send the warm offers and one community post a week.
  - How fast our agents can produce a Leak Report (meter report #1).
  - The real reply rate on the free-report offer (kill test 1 measures it).
**Recommendation: go with N1 "Store Leaks".** Weeks 1–2:
1. Scene pack + 4 Shorts.
2. Free checklist + email capture.
3. The Leak Report pipeline; run report #1 free for one of Nathan's clients.
4. Nathan sends the "free report" offer to 10 Cobundle merchants.
5. Playbook build starts only after **20 sign-ups or 3 paid reports** (the pre-sale gate).
