## 2. Remaining weak points in v2 (not fatal; the plan must carry them)
1. **D's engine is route 1 (Nathan sends videos), and nobody knows if he does outbound at all.** NATHAN-INPUTS #1 and #3 are still pending. At R2's cold rates, 1 close/month needs ~650 touches; that's impossible in 2–3 h/week. So D only works if Nathan already has a warm pipeline (prospects, referrals, existing clients) that the videos slot into. **This is the first question for Nathan, before anything is built.**
2. **The sensitivity table's dates assume no churn and start in Jan 2027.** That's fine as a scenario, but AI-receptionist churn among small local businesses is commonly high [M]; read the "$10k Jun 2027" row as optimistic.
3. **"Videos lift reply rate" is the whole thesis and it has no outside evidence** (FC: no independent case of 3D/low-poly Shorts converting local businesses; video-in-email 4.9% vs 3.9% is one vendor study [W]). Good: v2 makes it the first kill test. Tighten it: the test needs a **same-week A/B on Nathan's own prospects** (half get the video link, half plain text), not a comparison to a remembered baseline.
4. **Facts-only rule vs this niche.** Lead-response (Oldroyd/InsideSales), Baymard and Portent are citable; "1 missed call = a lost patient worth hundreds" and the booking-form stat have no source yet. v2 says "build only if sourced". Hold that line; the channel's credibility is the sales asset.
5. **Public channel = near-zero contribution.** ~20k views/month × 0.0025–0.1% = 0.5–20 clicks. Fine, but then don't spend scarce token budget on daily public posting. **3 videos/week, all reusable as sales assets**, is enough.
6. **Permit fine print (not scored, one line for the lead):** the fact-check found the permit is held by a natural person with a sole-establishment licence that carries a media activity, and Gulf News lists a no-"pseudonym user" duty. If Nathan's licence is an LLC, or the account is faceless, have his PRO confirm once. He says it's solved; this is just a check.
