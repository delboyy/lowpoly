# "The Leak": first 3 scripts (2026-10-29), for Nathan's review before building

Format: low-poly, number in line 1, twist, loop ending (the last line runs into line 1). 30–35 s each. Every video ends on one of Nathan's fixes, with a tracked link in the caption/DM.

---

## 1. "The 5-minute rule" → sells the **AI receptionist**
- **Hook bar:** Call back in 5 minutes, not 30
- **Script:**
  - Call a new lead back in five minutes instead of thirty, and you're a hundred times more likely to reach them.
  - That's from a study of thousands of real sales leads.
  - But most businesses call back hours later. Some never do.
  - [lowered] So who actually wins the customer?
  - Not the best business. The fastest one.
  - [quiet] So next time your phone rings while you're busy, remember:
  - *(loops to line 1)*
- **Figures:** 5 min · 30 min · 100×
- **Visuals:** a low-poly phone ringing on an empty shop counter; a clock face racing from 5 to 30; a customer figure walking out the door to a rival shop next door; the phone ringing again (loop).
- **Source to label on screen:** "MIT/InsideSales lead response study, 2007." It's vendor-sponsored, and it measured *reaching* a lead, not closing.
- **Caption CTA:** "Never miss the 5 minutes: our AI receptionist answers every call. Link / DM 'CALL'."

## 2. "7 in 10 carts" → sells **Cobundle** (Shopify) and **websites**
- **Hook bar:** 7 in 10 shoppers leave without paying
- **Script:**
  - Seven out of ten online shoppers fill their cart, then leave without paying.
  - That's the average across dozens of studies.
  - [lowered] So what scares them off?
  - It's not your price. The number one reason is surprise costs at checkout: shipping, taxes, fees.
  - [quiet] Show the full price early, and fewer walk away. Because right now,
  - *(loops to line 1)*
- **Figures:** 7 in 10 · #1 reason
- **Visuals:** a low-poly shop with 10 shoppers; 7 carts roll back to the shelf; a receipt grows extra lines (SHIPPING, TAX, FEE); the carts return (loop).
- **Sources:**
  - Baymard Institute, average documented cart-abandonment rate ~70% across 49 studies.
  - Baymard checkout survey: "extra costs too high" is the top reason (~48%).
  - Both get re-checked against Baymard's page at build time.
- **Caption CTA:** "Bundles that raise cart value: Cobundle for Shopify. Link."

## 3. "One second" → sells **websites**
- **Hook bar:** A slow website loses 2 in 3 sales
- **Script:**
  - A website that loads in one second can convert three times better than one that takes five.
  - [lowered] So where do the other customers go?
  - Back to Google. One tap, and they're on your competitor's page.
  - They never even see your offer.
  - [quiet] And it all comes down to this:
  - *(loops to line 1)*
- **Figures:** 1 s · 3× · 5 s
- **Visuals:** a low-poly phone with a loading spinner; a customer figure tapping back; the rival's shop lighting up; the spinner again (loop).
- **Source:** Portent 2019 study of B2B sites (1 s vs 5 s conversion ≈ 3×). On screen: "B2B sites, Portent 2019."
- **Hook bar check:** "2 in 3" is my arithmetic from "3× better": a site converting a third as well loses about 2 in 3 of the sales the fast site makes. QA must confirm the framing before it's used.
- **Caption CTA:** "Fast sites that sell. DM 'FAST'."
