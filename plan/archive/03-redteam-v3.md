## 2. Five weakest assumptions in line 1 (the "engine")
1. **Low-poly fits app promos.** In my fact-check, 7 of 14 Shopify listings had a video and **none was metaphor or 3D**: all UI, talking-head or 2D motion. Apps without a video may simply not value one; Growave (1,204 reviews, funded) skipping it is likely a choice, not a gap. → **Test the look before the outreach:** the Cobundle video plus 3 mocks shown to 3 developers Nathan knows, by 2026-11-20.
2. **$750 intro.** The one anchor is a single Dribbble seller. Market evidence: Fiverr 3D median ~$150, Upwork SaaS demo ~$400, Ministudio $449.99. **Intro at $450–500 for the first 2**, then $750–1,200 once a case study exists.
3. **Mock cost ~15 min each.** That's a personalised mock of *their* UI from one template, untested. Meter the first 3; if a mock takes >1 agent-hour, send the generic Cobundle video plus one still instead.
4. **Nathan sends 10 a week and handles replies, calls, briefs and revisions** inside 2–3 h/week, alongside his client work. The plan's own deciding unknown. If he won't, line 1 is marketplace inbound only (1/1).
5. **Delivery at 4–8 agent-hours per video.** Unmetered against the tight token budget; the Cobundle video is the meter. If it costs more than ~15% of $750, raise the price or simplify the template.

Also check: a 60–90 s promo + 15 s cutdown + 3 stills + 1 revision is a lot of scope for $750. Cap the stills and the cutdown as add-ons.


## 2b. Is "~10 mocks a week" realistic? No. Plan on 5.
| Cost per mock | Agents | Nathan |
|---|---|---|
| Claimed in v3 | ~15 min each, from a template | "approve and send" |
| Realistic [A, meter it] | **30–45 min each:** read their listing, write a 1-line script, swap name, colours and UI panel, render (~5 min, competing for the 3-render Mac slot), then a QA pass under our house rules | **12–15 min each:** find the contact route (support email, Partners profile, X), write a personal 3-line note, send, and follow up once |
| **At 10/week** | **5–7.5 agent-hours/week**: the equivalent of 5–7 full kit videos, on top of the Cobundle video, client clips and Leak Shorts. It likely blows the "tight" weekly token budget | **2–2.5 h/week**: all of his 2–3 h, leaving nothing for replies, calls, briefs, delivery approvals or his client work |
| **At 5/week** | ~2.5–4 agent-hours | ~1–1.25 h, leaving ~1–2 h for replies and delivery |

**Fixes:**
- **Tier the sends.** Make personalised 10 s video mocks only for the top ~10 targets. Everyone else gets the Cobundle video plus **one personalised still** (a ~5 min render).
- **Batch it.** Agents prepare 5 sends on Monday: contact route found, note drafted, mock attached. Nathan approves and sends them in one 30–45 min sitting.
- **Meter the first 3 mocks** (tokens/$ and minutes). If one costs more than ~1 agent-hour, stop making video mocks and use stills only.

The gates in §3 already assume ~5 a week (20 by 2026-12-15, 40 by 2027-01-31). The reply arithmetic stays the same, so expected app sales over 90 days stay ~0.2–0.4: line 1 is the upside, not the base.
