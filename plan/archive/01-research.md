# 01 Research: how faceless / 3D explainer channels make money, buyer-intent niches, funnel maths, UAE rails
Researcher, round 1. Dated 2026-10-29. Method: ~20 web searches + 6 page fetches. **Evidence grades:** [P] primary/official page I fetched; [S] secondary (news/law-firm); [W] weak (vendor blog, guru, SEO site, no method); [M] my own estimate/knowledge, not sourced this session. Almost all "how much do creators earn" numbers online are [W]. Treat accordingly.

## 0. Headline findings (read this if nothing else)
1. **No verified example of a low-poly 3D Shorts channel with disclosed non-ad revenue was found.** The only well-documented animated-explainer business is Kurzgesagt (2D, 20M+ subs), and its mix is *shop 40%, ads 13%, Patreon 9%, commercial sponsors 12%, institutional sponsors 10%* over ~8 years ([video summary](https://videohighlight.com/v/1x-i9z617z4) [W-S], consistent with [their 2023 statement](https://lr.eu.psf.lt/r/kurzgesagt/comments/10jlyyk/kurzgesagt_statement_to_the_conflict_of_interest/j7qqg8g)). Ad share is a minority even for the biggest. Products + sponsors dominate, but at 20M subs, not at our size.
2. **Selling the production skill (services) has the shortest path to $ at our audience size.** Market prices for a 60 s animated explainer: freelancers $500-3,000, boutique studios $3-8k, agencies $5-15k+ ([videoexplainers.com](https://videoexplainers.com/blog/how-much-does-explainer-video-cost) [W], [pixune](https://pixune.com/blog/animated-explainer-video-cost/) [W]); 3D adds 2-3x over motion graphics. Cheapest Fiverr 3D gigs start ~$80 for 20 s ([listing](https://fiverr.com/animationsbydot/create-marketing-2d-or-3d-explainer-animation-and-sales-video) [W]). Our edge: code-rendered scene kit, ~1 h/video, so even $150-400 per 30-60 s clip is a good margin. Needs a portfolio and outbound, not views.
3. **Shorts ad revenue is negligible for money-planning:** finance-ish Shorts RPM claims range $0.08-0.40 per 1k views, entertainment $0.01-0.10 ([fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-niche-selection-income-2026), [kineclip](https://kineclip.com/blog/youtube-rpm-by-niche-faceless/) [W]); our own memory file says $0.03-0.10. 1M Shorts views ≈ $30-400. Do not build the plan on YPP.
4. **UAE gating items:** (a) Stripe UAE needs a UAE trade licence or freelancer permit; individuals without one are not supported [P/S]. (b) Lemon Squeezy lists UAE under bank payouts [P]. (c) Amazon.ae pays only to UAE-dirham bank, reportedly [S, Feb 2024]. (d) A UAE **Advertiser Permit** has been mandatory since 2026-02-01 for anyone making promotional content from the UAE, paid or unpaid; affiliate promotion of other firms' products appears to be in scope [S]. (e) Finance/crypto content is a separate SCA "finfluencer" regime [S]. **Action for Nathan: get a freelancer permit / advertiser permit status checked before any affiliate or sponsor revenue.**

**Illustrative chain at 100k views/month (all assumptions, base-case low):**
100k views x 1% profile visit = 1,000 visits x 1% link click = **10 clicks**. At 3% purchase = 0.3 sales. A $29 product = **~$9/month**. Even the mid case (3% visits, 5% click, 2% buy) = 3,000 x 5% = 150 clicks x 2% = 3 sales ≈ $87. **To reach $1k/month via bio-link on a $29 product needs roughly 40-100x current view levels (≈ 1M+ views/month), or a better CTA than the bio link (DM keyword automation at 12-18%, email list, comments, pinned comment/YouTube description).** Services avoid this: one $300 sale needs a handful of qualified DMs/emails, not views.

### Rules
- **UAE Advertiser Permit (UAE Media Council / NMA):** mandatory from **2026-02-01** for *every individual who creates advertising content from within the UAE* (citizens, residents, visitors), **paid or unpaid**; free for the first 3 years for residents (1-year, renewable) [S: [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/new-uae-law-advertiser-permit-now-mandatory-for-influencers-and-creators-for-social-media-1.500427938)]. Fines: operating without a permit AED 10,000 first offence, 40,000 repeat; content breaches AED 5,000-1,000,000; misleading info AED 5,000-10,000. Exemption: promoting your **own** products/services on personal accounts (so selling our own products/services is likely exempt; affiliate for others is not). Other outlets report different fee/fine figures [W]: confirm with the regulator.
- **Finance/crypto:** SCA "finfluencer" licence launched May 2025 (Chairman Resolution No. 10/2025) covering social content giving financial recommendations on regulated products or virtual assets; SCA-licensed professionals excluded; DIFC has separate DFSA financial-promotion rules [S: [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/uae-new-licensing-regime-finfluencers), [ATB Legal](https://atblegal.com/blog/uaes-new-compliance-framework-for-finfluencers/), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/your-money/saving-investment/finfluencers-in-uae-guidelines-to-follow-before-promoting-financial-products-1.500166583)]. A "1,000+ followers" threshold appears in one commercial blog only [W]. VARA crypto-marketing rules: not found. **Conclusion: keep any finance content purely educational (no product links, no calls to trade) or avoid it.**
- Unverified, flag for Red Team: UAE freelancer permit costs/timing; whether selling digital products from a UAE resident without a licence is allowed (Stripe says licence needed; Lemon Squeezy/Gumroad as merchant-of-record may sidestep, but UAE commercial-activity law was not researched).

## 5. What this implies for the Strategist (evidence-based, not a plan)
1. Candidate engines with non-view-dependent revenue: **(a) service** (3D explainer clips for SaaS/ecommerce/property), **(b) affiliate + product in B2B/SMB or ecommerce**, **(c) sponsor/lead-gen later**. (a) beats (b) on time-to-first-dollar; (b) beats (a) on passive scale.
2. Model funnels with the **low** column in section 3; the bio link is the weakest conversion step; plan for email capture, DM keywords and comments.
3. **Hard gate:** UAE freelancer/advertiser permit (and Stripe eligibility) before any affiliate/sponsor/Stripe revenue. Lemon Squeezy is the probable first store rail.
4. Do not count: YPP ads, Whop original-content campaigns, stock licensing. Treat as optional upside experiments.
