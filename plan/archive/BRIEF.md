## Hard facts and constraints
- **Production:** code-rendered low-poly 3D Shorts (Three.js/Remotion), ~1 h per video once a scene kit exists, AI voice, no music, no faces, every claim sourced. We can also make 16:9 explainers and stills.
- **Results so far:** one breakout-ish post (penny, 1,690 IG views); everything else 100–220. Accounts have very few followers.
- **Nathan is in the UAE:** no IG/TikTok creator payouts. YouTube YPP is available (apply before 2027-01-31). Payout rails to a UAE bank must be confirmed for any store.
- **Regulatory:** avoid promoting credit, investing or crypto products (FCA risk found earlier; UAE rules unchecked).
- **Nathan:** does logins, payments and account settings; runs other businesses (web/AI-receptionist agency, Shopify app work); limited time; a tight weekly AI-token budget, so agents should work efficiently.
- **Assets that can be sold:** the videos themselves (licensing), the production skill (service), an audience (sponsors, affiliates, products), data/spreadsheets behind stories, the 3D kit/templates.

## What the plan must answer
1. **Niche and angle:** which audience and topic, in low-poly style, has *buyer intent* (they would click and pay), not just curiosity? Is "money stories" right, or something else (e.g. a B2B-adjacent niche, a hobby with products, education)?
2. **Revenue engine:** what exactly do we sell or link, to whom, at what price, through which funnel (bio link, landing page, email, DM)? Rank options by realistic $/month at our actual view levels, time to first dollar, and Nathan's time required.
3. **Path to $10k:** milestones (first $1, first $100, $1k/mo, $10k) with dates, the content cadence, and what must be true at each step. Show the arithmetic and assumptions.
4. **What to build now** (week 1–2) and what Nathan must do himself.
5. **Kill criteria:** what signal, by when, tells us to change course.
