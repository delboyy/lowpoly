## 2. Line 1 in detail: the only line that reaches $10k on its own
- **Offer:**

  | Tier | What's included | Price |
  |---|---|---|
  | Promo | 60–90 s low-poly promo + 15 s ad cutdown + 3 stills, 1 revision | **$750** intro (first 3 clients), then **$1,200** |
  | Long cut | Shopify's 2–3 min length | +$400 |

  Anchors: the Dribbble low-poly seller at $750; Contra 3D 30–60 s median ~$2,250 [H-A].
- **Why they'd buy:** 60% of the sampled listings have no video. Mid-size apps (300–1,499 reviews) are 79% without [P H-A], and Shopify tells developers to add a promotional one [P]. The seller is another app developer with a live example on his own listing.
- **Honest gap:** no evidence that a video lifts installs (H-A §4). We sell "look like a top app", not a guaranteed install lift. Cobundle's own before/after numbers (line 0) become our only proof. Collect them from week 1.
- **Arithmetic:** `sales = mocks sent × reply rate × close rate`.
  - Reply 2–6% (R2; the one free-mockup case is 6.2% [W]).
  - Close from a reply: 1 in 3–5, peer-to-peer and warm-ish [A].
  - 40 mocks over 90 days (~4 h/month of agent time): 40 × 4% × 25% ≈ **0.4 sales**, range 0.2–1.0 → **$0–750**. The rest of the 90-day range comes from Nathan's peers (devs he already knows), where the reply rate is higher.
  - For **$10k**: ~9 sales at ~$1,100 average → ~900 cold mocks at 4% × 25%, or ~45 warm conversations at 20% [A]. Realistic pace: 1 sale/month from month 3 → **$10k ≈ Q4 2027**. With a Cobundle case study and referrals at 2/month, **mid-2027**.
- **Delivery:** custom app scenes (their UI as a stylised low-poly panel at ≤25% of the runtime, the rest metaphor) take ~4–8 agent-hours [A]. Meter the first one, and cap revisions at 1.

## 3. Cobundle (lines 0 and 3): branches on Nathan's unknowns
`Cobundle MRR added per month = new paying installs/month × price`. Base: lines 0 + 3 add 1–5 tracked paying installs a month [A]; there is no evidence either way.
| Cobundle price → | 1 install/mo | 3 installs/mo | 5 installs/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| $10/mo | +$10/mo each month (90 d: ~$60) | ~$180 | ~$300 |
| $20/mo | ~$120 | ~$360 | ~$600 |
| $30/mo | ~$180 | ~$540 | ~$900 |
(90-day total, assuming installs accrue from day 1 with no churn.) **Cobundle alone cannot reach $10k in 12 months** below ~10 paying installs a month at $30. It is a compounding side line, and its listing video is mainly the portfolio piece for line 1.
**Branch on client work (line 2):** if Nathan has 1 client, the 90-day range is $0–600; with 3–5 clients, $400–1,800. If his clients pay a monthly fee, the clip is pitched as a one-off add-on, not a fee change.

- **Line 1:** by **2027-01-31**, if ≥30 mocks have been sent and there are **<2 replies** → switch to marketplace inbound only. If **0 sales by 2027-02-28** → stop line 1.
