## B. Reply within an hour: numbers right after v5's correction; fix the comparison, one line and one visual
Facts (HBR, Oldroyd/McElheran/Elkington, Mar 2011):
- **1.25M leads at 42 US firms** (29 B2C, 13 B2B). Firms that tried to contact a lead within an hour were **nearly 7× as likely to qualify it**, i.e. have a meaningful conversation with a decision maker, **as firms that tried even an hour later**. The figure was >60× against waiting 24 h or more.
- **Separate audit of 2,241 US firms:** 37% responded within an hour, 16% in 1–24 h, 24% after more than 24 h, and **23% never responded**. The mean response time was 42 h.
- Both are **web leads**, not phone calls.

## C. Property inquiries: the figure is right, but the meaning needs one word; label the sponsor
Facts (WAV Group, "Agent Responsiveness Study", PDF dated 2013, published Jan 2014; read directly):
- **Sample:** 384 inquiries to 384 different brokers/agents, 11 US states, sent through broker sites, Zillow, Trulia and Realtor.com ("inquire about this listing" forms), 50% with a phone number. Sized for 95% confidence ±5%.
- **Result:** **48% of buyer inquiries were never responded to.** 52% got a response, with an average response time of 5.3 h.
- **Important caveat:** **auto-responders were not counted.** "All of the portals and most broker websites have auto-responders." So 48% got **no reply from an agent**, though many will have received an automatic email.
- **Sponsor:** done **for Weichert**, a brokerage that benchmarked itself against the industry (bias risk). Grade: B for the method, C for independence.
- The report also says "68% of customers are most inclined to work with the first professional they speak with", but gives no source. **Don't use it.**

| Line | Problem | Fix (exact) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook bar "Almost half of home-buyer inquiries got no reply" | Needs "from an agent" because auto-replies were excluded. | "**Almost half of home-buyer inquiries never got a reply from an agent**" |
| "Almost half of the people who asked an estate agent about a home never heard back." | It counts inquiries, not people, and "never heard back" ignores the auto-replies. | "**Almost half of buyers who asked about a home online never heard back from an agent.**" |
| "In one US study, forty-eight percent of buyer inquiries got no reply at all." | "At all" is false (auto-replies). | "In one US study of 384 agencies, forty-eight percent of buyer inquiries **never got a reply from a person.**" |
| "To whichever agent answered first." | Unsourced (the only support is the uncited 68% claim). | "**Probably to the next agent who did.**" |
| "And a home is the biggest thing most people will ever buy." | A common claim, unsourced, but harmless. | Optional: "And a home is a big-ticket purchase." Keep it if you like; it isn't a figure. |
| "One unanswered message can cost a whole commission." | A logical "can", not a statistic. | Keep. |
| On screen "US study, WAV Group 2014" | Add the date context; the data is 12 years old. | "**US study, 2013–14 (WAV Group for Weichert)**" |
| Caption | Add "from an agent". | "In one US study, 48% of home-buyer inquiries never got a reply from an agent. Want to know how fast your agency replies? DM LEAK for a free check." |
