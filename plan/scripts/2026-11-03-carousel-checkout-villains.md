# Carousel: Meet the 4 villains hiding in your checkout
Slot: carousel 09:00 UTC. The date is a placeholder until Nathan approves a carousel style. Audience: Shopify / online stores → sells the Shopify sprint and "Fast Site".
Style: **villain line-up**, matching the villain Shorts Nathan picked (the Empty Chair, the Loading Spinner). Each slide is one character with a quote and one sourced fact.
Format: 7 slides, 1080×1350. **4 figures** (7 in 10, nearly 1 in 5, 0.1 s, +8.4%). "Top reason" is written in words so it isn't a fifth figure.
