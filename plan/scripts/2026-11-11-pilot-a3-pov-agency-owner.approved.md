Slot: tentative. Pilot from research/FORMATS-TRENDS-2026-11.md (top 5 #5, the funny arm). Sells **First Reply**.
Look: **our existing low-poly characters**. I don't think a new look is needed: an owner figure at a desk, a laptop, an inbox, a portal screen and a phone are all props we have or can make easily. No new style to approve.
