# Pilot A2: "Take two", missed-call roleplay (evergreen)
Slot: tentative. Pilot from research/FORMATS-TRENDS-2026-11.md (top 5 #1). This is the **evergreen** version; a National Day variant can follow (swap the time to a holiday banner and confirm the holiday dates first). Sells **instant text-back / First Reply**.
Voices: **NARRATOR** (dry, calm) and **CALLER** (stressed, then relieved). The voicemail greeting is read by the NARRATOR voice in a cheerful "recording" tone (or a third voice if the editor prefers).
Figures: **1 sourced** (27%, Invoca, already Red-Team checked in BL #6).
**AED counter:** the brief asked for an AED-lost counter, but we have no sourced job value for UAE trades. Rather than invent one, the counter reads **"Missed calls: 1 · Job value: AED ???"** and the VO tells viewers to fill in their own average job. Flagged for the lead.

Length: ~98 words, ~35 s (plus ring/beep beats).

## For the Red Team

- Caption: "not to the next number on the list" implies the caller would otherwise try someone else. Soften to "keeps the customer talking to you"?
- Is "AED ???" OK as a fill-in-your-own counter? Lead: is it acceptable for the brief?
- "Customers lost: 0" in take two describes the illustrative scene, not a result.
- "Al Barsha" is a neighbourhood, not a business.
