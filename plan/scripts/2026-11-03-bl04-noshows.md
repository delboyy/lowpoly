Other styles (deadpan, mock doc) are in `agents/script-writer/drafts/bl-04-noshows.md`.

## How to build a test post
Pick **one hook** (A/B/C) and **one script** (S or W). The hook replaces line 1, and its matching loop ending replaces the last words of the last line so the loop still reads as one sentence.
Clean combinations against the control **A + S**: `B + S`, `C + S` (hook test) and `A + W` (script test). Avoid `B/C + W`, which changes two things.

## For the Red Team
- Is the gloss "nearly one in five" OK for 18.8%? (An outside opinion said yes.)
- The VO doesn't say who bears the $196. If a payer must be named, use "the health system", not "the clinic".
- "Someone else could have had that slot" (S) and "Nobody comes" (W) are scenes, not claims.
- "A reminder the day before / an easy way to rebook" is advice, not a measured result.
