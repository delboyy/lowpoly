## For the Red Team (same for a, b, c)
- **The hook "Your checkout lost the sale"** (the lead's line): Baymard notes that an abandoned cart isn't always a lost sale (many shoppers are browsing). Keep it as a slogan, or use the fallback "Your checkout lost the cart."
- The fix lines (full price early, guest checkout) are advice; "show the full price early" was accepted on BL #1.
- The joke lines are about the checkout process only.
