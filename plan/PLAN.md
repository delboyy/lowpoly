**Brief (Nathan):** niche low-poly content, with things sold alongside it. Don't niche the offer to Shopify: you build websites, AI automations, integrations and custom software with AI.
Detail: `01-research-v5.md` (Researcher), `02-strategy-v5.md` (Strategist), `03-redteam-v5.md` (Red Team). This replaces v4 "Store Leaks", which lives on as one series.

## The angle
- **Content:** "Business Leaks", low-poly Shorts in the penny format (number hook, twist, loop) about where businesses quietly lose money or time. Every video ends on a fix you build.
- **Two series:**
  - **Front-Desk Leaks:** missed calls, slow replies to inquiries, no-shows. Aimed at real estate, trades and clinics.
  - **Store Leaks:** carts, speed, bundles. Aimed at Shopify stores; your 2 clients and Cobundle are the proof.
- **Split:** 2 of every 3 Shorts go to whichever business type you sell to; 1 goes to Store Leaks.
- **Name:** keep **@lineitemfyi**, with "Business Leaks" as the series. Both agents agree; a rename loses penny's history for no proven gain.

## The hard truth from the research
- **Videos won't bring your first clients.** At ~1k views, expect about 0 inbound in 90 days. Clients 1–3 come from **people you know and face-to-face in the UAE**, on WhatsApp or in person.
- **The videos are your sales samples.** You send them, with a free report, to people you know. They become an inbound engine only over 6–18 months.
- **Almost nobody makes animated content about business leaks.** That gap is real.

**Why text first, voice later:**
- **UAE numbers:** voice AI tools can't easily get UAE numbers.
- **Forwarding cost:** forwarding a du landline abroad costs the client ~$0.45/min, about $225/month at 500 minutes.
- **Recording consent:** recording a call needs consent under UAE law (Art. 44).
- **Thin margin** at $250/month.

WhatsApp follow-up is cheap (the first 1,000 messages a month are free) and allowed for single-business bots. Real estate already has a rival, Sabbaq ($199–799/mo), so we win on **set up in person, wired into your WhatsApp and CRM, Arabic**.
