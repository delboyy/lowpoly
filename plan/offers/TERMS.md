# Service terms: one page (DRAFT for Nathan to approve; 2026-10-30)
Plain-English terms for First Reply, Show Up, One Pipe and Never Miss (voice, later). They go into the quote or invoice, and the client accepts them in writing (a WhatsApp "agreed" or a signed PDF). **This is not legal advice: have a UAE lawyer read it once before client #1**, especially §5 and §6.

**1. Who.** "We" = Nathan's licensed business [trade name, licence no.]. "You" = the client business named on the quote.

**2. What we build (scope).** Only what the quote lists, for example:
- **First Reply:** inquiries from up to 3 named sources → an instant WhatsApp/email reply from approved templates → logged in your CRM → up to 3 chase messages until the lead replies or books.
- **Show Up:** reminders and a rebooking link.
- **One Pipe:** one trigger → one destination.

Anything else (new sources, new languages, custom reports) is a separate quote. The setup includes **1 round of changes** within 14 days of go-live.

**3. What the automation does and doesn't do.**
- It sends pre-approved messages and answers simple FAQs about *your* business.
- It does not negotiate prices, give legal, medical or financial advice, or make promises on your behalf.
- **Anything it can't handle goes to you**: a WhatsApp/email alert to the person you name, with the lead's details.
- **You stay responsible for following up on alerts and for every deal.**

**4. Fallback to you.**
- If the automation, WhatsApp, the CRM or a provider is down, inquiries still reach you through the source's normal channel (portal email, your phone, your inbox). We never switch that channel off.
- **Voice (Never Miss):** unanswered or failed calls forward to the owner's phone you name.

**5. Disclosure: AI and recording.**
- Messages and calls say they are automated, e.g. "This is [Agency]'s automated assistant".
- **Calls (voice only):** the greeting says the call is recorded and handled by AI before any recording starts, and a caller can ask for a person. Calls are recorded only with that notice.
- You must not ask us to remove these notices.

**6. Data.**
- **You own your leads and customer data.** We act on your instructions only.
- The service uses third-party providers: Meta/WhatsApp, your CRM, the automation host and, for voice, the AI voice provider. Some store data outside the UAE.
- You confirm that you are allowed to contact the people who reach you, and that you will only send marketing to people who agreed to it.
- We keep access only while the service runs. On cancellation we export your data to you and delete our copies within 30 days.
- **No health or medical data** without a separate written agreement.

**7. Third-party rules and costs.**
- You hold your own WhatsApp Business account, number and CRM in your name (we help set them up).
- Meta can approve, reject or restrict templates and accounts at its own discretion. We are not liable for its decisions.
- **Included in the monthly fee:** the automation host and Meta's message fees up to the volume on the quote (First Reply intro: **200 new inquiries a month**). Above that, usage is billed at cost + 10%. **Voice:** includes [500] minutes a month; extra minutes are billed at [AED 1.50] per minute.

**8. No liability for missed business.**
- We set up and maintain the automation with reasonable care. We do not guarantee any number of leads, replies, bookings or sales.
- **We are not liable for missed leads, calls, jobs or appointments, or lost profit,** including those caused by outages at Meta, the CRM, the internet or the phone network.
- Our total liability is capped at the fees you paid in the last 3 months.
- If our setup is faulty, we fix it free.

**9. Prices and payment (intro, clients 1–3).**
- **First Reply:** $400 setup + $150/month (standard later: $1,200 + $150/month).
- **Show Up:** $400 + $75/month.
- **One Pipe:** $400–600 one-off.
- **Setup:** 50% to start, 50% at go-live.
- **Monthly:** invoiced in advance; the first month starts at go-live.
- The intro price holds for **12 months** from go-live. In return, **you agree to be a named case study** (a short low-poly video using your anonymised numbers; you approve it before it's used).
- Prices exclude VAT where it applies.

**10. Cancelling.**
- **Monthly:** either side can cancel with **30 days' notice** by WhatsApp or email. There's no lock-in and no refund for the month in progress.
- **Setup fees are non-refundable** after work starts.
- **Late payment:** if an invoice is unpaid 14 days after its due date, we can pause the service. Your inquiries still reach you through §4.

**11. Ownership.** You own the templates, your data and your accounts. We own our reusable workflows and code. You get a licence to use them while you pay, and a full export if you leave.

**12. Changes and law.** Changes are agreed in writing. UAE law applies, and the courts are [Dubai].
