## Bottom line for the decision
1. **Content is the proof and the slow channel; it will not produce the first 3-5 clients.** The one faceless-agency lead-gen claim found needed ~85k followers and 18 months (unverified blog). Organic content-to-client numbers do not exist. First clients come from Nathan's warm network, direct outreach by him, and referral partners.
2. **Lead verticals: home services/trades and dental/clinics (plus real-estate agencies as third).** They have the most vivid sourced "leak" stats, are phone/WhatsApp-driven, and the fix (AI receptionist / follow-up automation) is a few-hundred-dollar-a-month retainer. E-commerce keeps Baymard/Deloitte as shared hooks and Nathan's Shopify credibility.
3. **Realistic first-buyer price (no portfolio): $0-500 setup + $100-500/month** (all D). The v5 draft ladder ($195-295 Leak Report, $750-3,000 builds) sits at or above market for a no-reviews seller on websites (UK packaged median £520) but is plausible on automations/AI receptionist if sold to a warm contact.
4. **Competition in "animated business-leak storytelling for SMB owners" looks thin** (shallow yt-dlp pass; IG/TikTok unchecked). Faceless business channels exist but target a general audience (Alux 5.1M, PolyMatter 1.93M, Business Casual 1.15M, Practical Wisdom 1.04M subs) and monetise via ads/sponsors.

## Q5 input: effect of broadening on the numbers (Researcher's view)
- **Raises** the ceiling and the number of reachable buyers (large UAE SME base, many fix types). **Lowers** the odds that any single video's stats map to a buyer, and spreads Nathan's 2-3 h/week thin across five build types. A tight vertical (trades or clinics) with 1-2 productised fixes (AI receptionist + lead-follow-up automation) converts better than five offers; builder anecdotes say narrowing raised conversion [D].
- 90-day realism from evidence: 1-3 clients at $100-500/mo + $0-1,000 setup = low hundreds to ~$1-2k cash; this is a warm-outreach outcome, independent of views. Content-led inbound at ~1k-view levels: assume ~0 in 90 days.
