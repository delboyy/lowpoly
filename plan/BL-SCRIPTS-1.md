## A. "7 in 10 carts" (Store Leaks): sells the Shopify sprint and Cobundle
- **Hook bar:** 7 in 10 online carts never check out
- **Script:**
  - Seven out of ten online shoppers fill a cart, then leave without paying.
  - That's the average across dozens of studies.
  - [lowered] So what scares them off?
  - Not the price of the product. The number one reason is extra costs at checkout: shipping, taxes, fees.
  - [quiet] Show the full price early, and fewer walk away. Because right now,
  - *(loops to line 1)*
- **Figures:** 7 in 10 · #1 reason
- **Visuals:** a low-poly shop with a row of shoppers and carts. 7 of 10 carts roll back to the shelf. A receipt grows extra lines (SHIPPING, TAX, FEE) and the shopper walks out. The carts reset to the first frame.
- **Sources:**
  - Baymard Institute, cart abandonment rate (average of ~49 studies, 70.19%).
  - Baymard checkout survey: "extra costs too high" is the top reason (~48% of US shoppers who abandoned).
- **Caption:** "7 in 10 carts never check out. The #1 reason is a surprise at checkout. Want to know where your store leaks? DM LEAK for a free check."

## B. "Reply within an hour" (Front-Desk Leaks): sells "First Reply"
- **Hook bar:** Reply within an hour: 7× more likely to qualify
- **Script:**
  - Reply to a new lead within an hour, and you're about seven times more likely to turn it into a real sales conversation.
  - That's from more than a million leads at forty-two US companies.
  - But when researchers tested over two thousand companies, only about a third replied within the hour.
  - [lowered] And almost one in four never replied at all.
  - So the customer did what everyone does. They asked someone else.
  - [quiet] So if a message is sitting in your inbox right now, remember:
  - *(loops to line 1)*
- **Figures:** 7× · 1 in 3 · 1 in 4
- **Visuals:** a low-poly phone on a shop counter with a message bubble arriving. A clock face races past the hour mark. A row of 10 shop fronts: 3 light up "replied", and 2 stay dark with a "no reply" bubble. A customer figure walks to the next shop. A new message bubble arrives (loop).
- **Sources:**
  - Oldroyd, McElheran & Elkington, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads", HBR, March 2011. The 7× figure is from 1.25M leads at 42 US firms. The audit covered 2,241 US firms: 37% replied within an hour, and 23% never replied.
  - On screen: "HBR 2011".
- **Caption:** "Reply within an hour and you're ~7× more likely to qualify the lead (HBR, 2011). Want to know how fast your business replies? DM LEAK for a free check."

## C. "Half of property inquiries" (Front-Desk Leaks, real estate): sells "First Reply"
- **Hook bar:** Almost half of home-buyer inquiries got no reply
- **Script:**
  - Almost half of the people who asked an estate agent about a home never heard back.
  - In one US study, forty-eight percent of buyer inquiries got no reply at all.
  - [lowered] So where did those buyers go?
  - To whichever agent answered first.
  - And a home is the biggest thing most people will ever buy.
  - [quiet] One unanswered message can cost a whole commission. Because
  - *(loops to line 1)*
- **Figures:** 48%
- **Visuals:** a low-poly street of houses with FOR SALE signs. Message bubbles fly from a buyer figure to two agency shop fronts: one lights up and replies, the other stays dark. The buyer walks to the lit agency. A house key changes hands. Then a new bubble flies out (loop).
- **Source:** WAV Group, 2014 real-estate lead response study (48% of buyer inquiries got no response). On screen: "US study, WAV Group 2014". Red Team: confirm the exact figure and sample.
- **Caption:** "In one US study, 48% of home-buyer inquiries got no reply. Want to know how fast your agency replies? DM LEAK for a free check."
