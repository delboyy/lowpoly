# Logo brief for ChatGPT (2026-11-01)

Nathan: the current coin-and-drop avatar is out. He wants a **refined, professional logo, NOT low-poly**, for the profile pictures and the website. Nathan makes it in ChatGPT.
The videos stay low-poly; only the logo changes.

**Two marks, one family:**
1. **Business Leaks** (the show): the profile picture on IG @fixtheleak_ and YouTube.
2. **Sacred Scaling** (the company; the website is sacredscaling.com): the website header, favicon and share image.

Paste prompt 1 first. When you like a direction, ask for the tweaks at the bottom, then run prompt 2 in the **same chat** so the two marks match.

## Prompt 1: Business Leaks mark (profile picture)

```
Design a logo for "Business Leaks", a business-education brand that shows small businesses where they quietly lose money (missed calls, slow replies, abandoned carts) and then fixes it.

Style: refined, professional, modern flat vector logo, like a top design studio would make for a consulting firm. Clean geometric shapes, confident negative space, perfectly balanced. NOT 3D, NOT low-poly, NOT cartoon, no gradients, no shadows, no bevels, no clip art.

Concept: a single simple water drop whose shape also forms a second idea through negative space, for example an upward arrow or a check mark inside the drop ("the leak, found and fixed"). It must read clearly at 40 pixels wide.

Colours: deep teal #0E4F4C as the main colour, one small accent in warm orange-red #E2553B, on a warm cream background #F4E9D8. Two colours max plus the background.

Show 4 different directions side by side on one square image, each centred with plenty of empty space around it (it will be cropped to a circle). Icon only, no text.
```

## Prompt 2: Sacred Scaling logo (website), same chat

```
Now make the logo for the parent company, "Sacred Scaling", using the same design language, colours and line weight as the direction I picked.

Make: (a) the icon on its own, and (b) a horizontal lockup, the icon on the left and the words "Sacred Scaling" on the right in a clean modern sans-serif (like Inter or Satoshi, medium weight, slightly tight letter spacing). Spell it exactly: Sacred Scaling.

Flat vector, white or cream #F4E9D8 background, no mockups, no 3D.
```

## Tweaks to ask for if needed
- "Simpler. Fewer shapes; it has to work as a 32-pixel favicon."
- "Make the negative-space arrow/check clearer."
- "Show it in white on teal #0E4F4C as well."
- "Give me the same mark as a version with the wordmark 'Business Leaks' underneath."
- "Export the chosen one alone at 1024×1024, centred, nothing near the edges."

## Check before using it
- Shrink it to about 1 cm on screen. If you can't tell what it is, ask for "simpler".
- Check the spelling of any words letter by letter (ChatGPT sometimes swaps letters).
- Put the files in `brand/logo/` (any names) and tell the lead. The lead then:
  - makes the IG/YT profile sizes (1080 px; 800 px for YouTube);
  - has the website updated: header, favicon, apple-touch icon, share image. For a crisp website logo we'll redraw the chosen PNG as a clean SVG.
- Nathan uploads the profile pictures himself.
