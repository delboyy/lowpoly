We find the leak. Then we build the fix.

Sacred Scaling is a small studio run by Nathan, based in the UAE and working with businesses anywhere. We build websites, AI automations, integrations, custom software and Shopify fixes, and we use AI to build them fast.

Why "Business Leaks"?

Most small businesses don't lose money in one dramatic moment. It drips: an inquiry nobody answered on a Saturday, a call that rang out while the front desk was busy, a checkout that asked for one field too many.

Business Leaks is our show about those drips: short, low-poly videos, each built around one sourced number. It's on Instagram as @fixtheleak_. Sacred Scaling is the company behind it, and the one that builds the fixes.

How we work

  • Facts first. Every number we use has a source you can check. We don't invent figures about your business.
  • Small, fixed scope. One leak, one fix, a clear price before we start.
  • Your accounts, your data. Your WhatsApp, CRM and leads stay in your name. If you leave, you take everything with you.
  • Honest automation. Automated messages say they're automated, and anything they can't handle goes to a real person on your team.
