Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

This site is run by Sacred Scaling, a business licensed in the United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"). Business Leaks (Instagram @fixtheleak_) is our content series. This policy explains what personal data we collect through sacredscaling.com and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you request a free leak check

  • Your name, business name, website or Google Maps link, type of business, and the WhatsApp number or email you give us.
  • Anything you choose to write in the optional notes box.
  • Which page or post brought you here (for example "instagram / carts-video"), taken from the link you clicked, and the website that referred you, if any. We use this to learn which videos are useful.

When you browse

We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) that count page views without identifying you or tracking you across sites. This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To make your leak report: we read your business's public reviews and website.
  • To send you the report, and to reply if you respond to it. We won't add you to a mailing list or send marketing without asking first.
  • To understand which of our videos and pages bring people here.

Our legal basis is your request (you asked for the report) and our legitimate interest in running and improving this site.

Who else handles it

  • Netlify hosts this site and receives form submissions.
  • Cloudflare provides the page-view analytics.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) or our email provider, when we send you your report.

Some of these providers store data outside the UAE, including in the United States. We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep leak-check requests for up to 12 months, then delete them, unless you become a client, in which case we keep what we need to work together and to meet legal and tax obligations.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email {{email}} and we'll act on it within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
