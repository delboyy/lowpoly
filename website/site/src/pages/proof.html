The numbers behind the leaks

General findings, not facts about your business. Several come from companies that sell a fix for the same problem; we say so where that's the case.

~7×
Firms that tried to contact a web lead within an hour were nearly 7 times as likely to qualify it as firms that waited even an hour longer.1.25 million web leads at 42 US firms. The same paper's audit of 2,241 US firms found 37% responded within an hour and 23% never responded. Oldroyd, McElheran & Elkington, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads", Harvard Business Review, March 2011. Source
