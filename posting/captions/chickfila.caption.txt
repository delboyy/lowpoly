Chick-fil-A closes every Sunday and still sells more than twice as much per restaurant as McDonald's.

2025: average stand-alone Chick-fil-A $9.16M (2,302 non-mall restaurants, 2026 franchise disclosure, Item 19). McDonald's US: $51.9B franchised sales across 13,706 US restaurants (~95% franchised) ≈ $4.0M each (our arithmetic). The operator pays a $10,000 initial fee; Chick-fil-A's ongoing fees are 15% of gross receipts plus 50% of net profit.

Sources: Chick-fil-A 2026 FDD, Item 19 and fees (via franchisechatter.com); McDonald's 2025 10-K.
