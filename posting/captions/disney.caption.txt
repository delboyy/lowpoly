Disney's parks earn more than twice what its movies, TV shows and Disney+ make.

FY2025 segment operating income: Experiences (parks, resorts, cruises, consumer products) $10.0B; Entertainment (studios, TV networks, Disney+, Hulu) $4.7B; Sports $2.9B. Margins are our arithmetic: 27.6% vs 11.0%.

Sources: The Walt Disney Company FY2025 Q4 earnings release (SEC 8-K, 2025-11-13).
