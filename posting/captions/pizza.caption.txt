Two pizzas. Now worth $800 million.

On 22 May 2010, Laszlo Hanyecz paid 10,000 bitcoin for two pizzas, then worth about $41. It's recognised as the first commercial bitcoin transaction. At about $80,400 per bitcoin (28 Oct 2026), those coins are worth roughly $800 million. He has said he has no regrets.

Sources: Guinness World Records, "First commercial Bitcoin transaction"; Decrypt; Bloomberg interview via Decrypt; BTC price: CoinGecko spot, 28 Oct 2026. Not financial advice.
