American Express paid Delta more last year than Delta made in pre-tax profit.

2025: American Express remuneration $8.2B (+11%); Delta income before taxes $6.2B; operating revenue $63.4B; passenger revenue +2%. Amex's payments are revenue, not profit: they cover SkyMiles and other card benefits, and Delta later flies the award seats.

Sources: Delta Air Lines 2025 10-K.
