America lost money on every penny it made.

Each penny cost about 3.7¢ to make and distribute (US Mint FY2024 annual report: $0.0369), and the Mint shipped about 3.17 billion that year. The last circulating pennies were struck in Philadelphia on 12 Nov 2025, ending production that began in 1793. The US Treasurer said ending production saves taxpayers $56 million. Existing pennies remain legal tender.

Sources: US Mint 2024 Annual Report (via NBC News coverage); AP coverage of the final strike, 12 Nov 2025.
