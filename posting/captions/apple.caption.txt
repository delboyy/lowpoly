Apple's services are a quarter of its sales, but over forty percent of its gross profit.

FY2025: services $109.2B of $416.2B sales (26%); services gross margin $82.3B of $195.2B (42%). Apple keeps 75.4% of each services dollar vs 36.8% on products. Mac + iPad sales: $61.7B. Gross profit, before operating costs.

Sources: Apple FY2025 10-K.
